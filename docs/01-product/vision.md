# Product vision

Status: Draft

Help a learner capture useful knowledge, find it later, and ask questions grounded in their own material. The core loop is capture → organize → retrieve → learn. Success means users can recover a saved idea and understand where an AI answer came from.

Confirmed audience: anyone with a Google account, including the owner and family. Confirmed: separate private accounts with Google sign-in. Each person's notes, references, search results and chats remain private. Public Google registration is confirmed; shared family knowledge is outside the initial MVP.

The product must remain useful when AI is unavailable: notes, references, search, and export are independent capabilities. Future learning tools build on this reliable foundation.

Measure task completion for capture/search, save failures and conflicts, citation validity, retrieval usefulness on a small evaluation set, and AI cost per successful answer. Numerical launch targets await workload and usability baselines.
